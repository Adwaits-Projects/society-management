Society Rules & Regulations

Community Guidelines for Harmonious Living

At The SkyLine Township, we believe in fostering a respectful, safe, and harmonious community. Please review and adhere to the following guidelines to ensure a premium living experience for all residents.

Community Guidelines

  1. No loud music or parties after 10:00 PM.
  2. All visitors must register at the security gate before entry.
  3. Keep society neat and clean.
  4. Residents must park vehicles only in allotted parking spaces.
  5. Speed limit inside the society premises is 20 km/h.
  6. Pets must be kept on a leash in common areas.
  7. Wet and dry waste must be segregated before disposal.
  8. Swimming pool timings are from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
  9. Children below 12 years must be supervised in play areas.
  10. Fire exits, staircases and emergency routes must remain unobstructed.
  11. Clubhouse bookings require prior approval from management.
  12. Damaging common property may result in penalties.
  13. Any maintenance issue should be reported through the Resident Portal.
  14. Construction or renovation work is permitted only during approved hours.
  15. Water and electricity should be used responsibly.
  16. Residents must comply with local municipal and housing society regulations.
